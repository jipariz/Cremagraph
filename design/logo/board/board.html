What we heard

The brief

An app that films an espresso shot with its shot graph (pressure, flow, weight) drawn over it, for home baristas who tune their machines and share their shots. It should feel precise, crafted and warm, and it has to work as an app icon on Android, iOS, the desktop and the web.

precisecraftedwarmshareable

Success: says filming plus graph without a coffee-cup cliché, reads at 16 px, works in one colour, keeps the app's orange on espresso.

Concept A

Viewfinder · Playhead

A camera's four corners frame the shot as it is recorded: the curve is drawn up to a playhead, and the dot rides the playhead where the curve meets it.

  • Says what the app does, recording the shot with its graph, without drawing a cup
  • Two colours with a meaning: the frame and playhead are the camera, the orange curve and dot are the shot
  • The dot sits on the line, so nothing floats above the curve to read as a head
