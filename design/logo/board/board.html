What we heard

The brief

An app that films an espresso shot with its shot graph (pressure, flow, weight) drawn over it, for home baristas who tune their machines and share their shots. The redesign ('Instrument') makes the app feel like a precision device: black, one signal orange, monospace numbers, square corners. The mark must match it and work as an app icon on Android, iOS, the desktop and the web.

precisetechnicalconfidentshareable

Success: keeps the recorded-trace idea, reads at 16 px, works in one colour, owns the signal orange on black, sits naturally in the Instrument screens.

Concept A

Signal tile

The live trace enters a solid signal tile from its edge and is cut out of it, up to the pen head that is writing it.

  • Keeps the old mark's idea, the shot recorded as its graph, and drops only the viewfinder corners
  • One colour and one outline: the ground shows through the cut, so it works on black, on white and in any single ink
  • Square, flat and monospaced like the Instrument screens; strongest of the three at 16 px
