What we heard

The brief

An app that films an espresso shot with its shot graph (pressure, flow, weight) drawn over it, for home baristas who tune their machines and share their shots. It should feel precise, crafted and warm, and it has to work as an app icon on Android, iOS, the desktop and the web.

precisecraftedwarmshareable

Success: says filming plus graph without a coffee-cup cliché, reads at 16 px, works in one colour, keeps the app's orange on espresso.

Concept A

Viewfinder

A camera's four corners frame the shot curve; the dot, set apart from its end, is now.

  • Says what the app does, filming the shot with its graph, without drawing a cup
  • Two colours with a meaning: the frame is the camera, the orange curve is the shot
  • A heavier small-size cut keeps the frame and the dot readable at 16 px
