SPLASH · SIGNAL TILE

The tile cuts in, the pen lands on its edge and plots the shot's trace across it, the stem drops, the head ticks once, and the mark hands off into the first screen's header mark. See SPEC.md for the numbers.

Android plays 0–1000 in the system splash on its own compressed timeline (wipe 0–200, land 180–260, draw 240–880, stem 880–1000); the app carries on with the tick and the hand-off. iOS starts from the finished mark (launch image) and plays only the tick and the hand-off. Desktop plays the full sequence and fades out (no header mark). The web loops the draw until the app is ready.
