OnlyShots films your espresso shot with its graph on top, drawn live from your machine and scale, showing the readings you pick. Export the video and share it.

RECORD A SHOT
• Connects to a Gaggiuino-modded machine over your Wi-Fi, and to a Bluetooth scale.
• Starts recording when the brew switch flips on and stops a few seconds after the shot, so your hands stay free.
• Keeps a few seconds from before the shot, so the first drops are on film.
• No machine? Use a scale on its own, or try the built-in sample shot.

PUT THE GRAPH WHERE YOU WANT IT
• Drag and resize the graph on the camera view.
• Pick what it shows: pressure, flow, weight, temperature, phases and shot time.
• Choose a built-in style or make your own: colours, lines, grid, readings, text, glass and blur.
• What you see in the preview is exactly what the export draws.

EDIT AFTER
• Pick any video you filmed and a shot file, and line them up: by the video's clock, by the pump's sound, or by hand.
• Trim, frame it in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9, and move the crop.
• Undo and redo every change.
• Import shots from your Gaggiuino's history, from .shot files, or from Visualizer (visualizer.coffee) by share code or link: any machine's shot, or a Beanconqueror brew.

EXPORT
• Save to your gallery in up to 4K, or share at 1080p to Instagram, WhatsApp, Messages and anywhere else.
• Keep the sound, or mute it.

WORKS WITH
• Machines: Gaggiuino.
• Scales: Acaia, Bookoo, Decent Scale, DiFluid, Eclair, EspressiScale, Felicita, iKape, Insmart, MyScale, Precisa, Timemore, Varia, WeighMyBru, Wilfa and more.

PHONES, TABLETS AND FOLDABLES
Layouts for phones, tablets and foldables, in portrait and landscape, including tabletop on a folding phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your videos, shots and settings stay on your device. OnlyShots talks only to the machine and scale you pick.

OnlyShots is an independent app, not affiliated with Gaggia, Gaggiuino or any scale maker. Names are used only to say what the app works with.
